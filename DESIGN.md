# Amend Design System

## Direction
Calm clinical editorial: bright architectural imagery, confident serif typography and fine navy rules. A full-width photograph leads, followed by deliberate changes in density, editorial treatment navigation, a spacious team section and a clear consultation form.

## Tokens
Navy #173A73; deep navy #0D2854; orange #F7941D; ivory #FAFAF7; charcoal #18202A; muted #667085; pale surface #EEF3F7. Orange buttons carry dark text. Instrument Serif headings; DM Sans body and interface. Letter spacing 0. Fixed responsive type steps. Radius 12px. Spacing based on 8px. Content max 1280px.

## Behavior
Native accessible form inputs and details accordions. Tactile buttons, one-time 400ms entrances, reduced-motion support. Sticky desktop header and mobile bottom actions reserve space. No false booking success; missing configuration explains that nothing has been sent.

## Photography
Original logo used as supplied. Concept imagery must be captioned as demo imagery, never attributed to the real clinic. Portrait and before/after treatments remain unmistakably demonstrative until real, approved assets are supplied.
