# Amend Dental Clinic

## Platform
web

## Purpose
A mobile-first dental landing page helping prospective patients understand the brand and request a consultation.

## Confirmed Facts
Name: Amend Dental Clinic. The supplied original logo is authoritative and must remain unchanged. Positioning: contemporary advanced dentistry with patient-focused care.

## Content Boundaries
This is a demo site. Treatments, clinicians, reviews, cases, policies, photography and contact details may use polished fictional sample data when each relevant area is clearly identified as demo content. No sample detail may be presented as verified clinic information. No patient submission may be silently discarded or falsely confirmed.

## Brand Commitments
Premium healthcare editorial direction, deep navy, controlled orange, off-white, refined serif and contemporary sans. WCAG-conscious mobile-first experience. Original design inspired only by the reference site's general reassurance and booking structure.
